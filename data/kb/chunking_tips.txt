Chunking divides documents into smaller pieces before embedding. Chunk size and overlap are major tuning knobs.
Large chunks preserve context but can include unrelated text. Small chunks increase precision but may lose context.
Overlap helps preserve meaning across boundaries.

A practical baseline is 50-120 words per chunk with 10-25 words overlap for plain text notes.
Always inspect top retrieved chunks manually for representative queries.

Evaluate retrieval separately from generation. Keep logs of query, retrieved chunks, and final answer.
This makes it easier to diagnose whether failures come from retrieval misses or generation behavior.
